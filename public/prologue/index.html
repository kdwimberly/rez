Below Terra

The cavern was a gash in the earth—a place where light was scared to go.

Six figures

They stood at its center, encircling a symbol carved into the stone floor.

“What if we fail? The rune—it’s never been tested. Not like this.”

“The portal will hold. It has to. We’ve worked too hard to back out now.”

Vorthas

“Quiet, both of you. We are not summoning Him. The portal is simply a path.”

The invocation

Rii’arvaal ekmor.

Stone answered first. A hairline wound opened across the cavern wall.

The wound widened. Dust rose against gravity. Then the wind came from nowhere.

The rune was no longer beneath them.
It was around them.

One voice vanished. Then another. The circle emptied until Vorthas stood alone.

And then—nothing.

HIS KINGDOM
IS CALLING.

End of Prologue

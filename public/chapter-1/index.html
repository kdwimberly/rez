Chapter One

Zevora I

Daesmarch · Morning. A lighter chapter theme of parchment, water, fern, and old gold.

Reading edition · Motion edition in production

Morning entered the fortress in narrow bands, catching the brass at the edge of every plate and leaving the long Daevor table divided between light and shadow.

Zevora sat in the light. This was not an accident.

Around her, breakfast proceeded with the rigid quiet of ceremony. Aldrick spoke of the arena as if the stones themselves had asked for their attendance. Natalia listened. Dameon smiled at the mention of the crowd. Somewhere beyond the high windows, water moved through the hidden systems of Daesmarch, turning wheels no one at the table would ever see.

“You will be there,” Aldrick said.

Zevora touched the glyph at her pendant and felt its familiar edges press into her thumb.

“I will not support what I do not believe in.”

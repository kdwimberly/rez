About the project

Novel. Illustration. Direction.

REZ is a fantasy novel built to be read on the page and directed through the screen—without turning its hand-drawn images into disposable video.

What is a motion novel?

A motion novel keeps prose central while using scroll, pacing, framing, and restrained movement to direct the reader’s attention. The illustration remains a keyframe; the webpage becomes the camera.

Is this the complete book?

REZ will publish serially. Each chapter receives a permanent reading route, with interactive motion editions expanding as artwork is completed.

Is the artwork permanent?

Yes. Production artwork is stored in this site’s canonical source repository and served from its own asset paths—not from temporary attachment links.

Who created REZ?

REZ is written and directed by Kyle Wimberly and developed through By Forge Studios.

Can I start with Chapter One?

You can, but the intended beginning is the Prologue. Its final reveal establishes the shadow hanging over everything that follows.

Lore of Terra

Material that expands the world without appearing directly in the chapters: systems, histories, beliefs, and records from the margins.

Water remembers

Terra’s machines do not wait for lightning. Cities are organized around pressure, current, elevation, and the patient labor of water. Aqueducts are arteries. Turbines are civic organs. To interrupt a channel is to interrupt a district’s life.

Etheralia resin

Known in common speech as Rez, etheralia resin is refined into controlled forms for medicine, industry, ritual, and escape. Its power does not arrive without consequence. Every culture in Terra has built a different moral language around its use.

The old runes

Modern rune craft favors repeatable outcomes. Older forms are less obedient. Their geometry behaves less like writing and more like an agreement—one whose other party may still be listening.

Field record: Daesmarch

The Daevor fortress capital presents itself as permanent: high stone, waterworks, banners, and bloodlines. But permanence is the favorite costume of a system already beginning to crack.
